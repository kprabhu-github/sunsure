ABOUT SUNSURE

Powering India’s energy independence

Founded in 2014, Sunsure Energy is the preferred renewable energy solutions provider for India’s leading businesses and utilities transitioning to green power. As a leading Independent Power Producer (IPP), Sunsure enables corporations across India to embrace clean energy with ease and confidence through long-term Power Purchase Agreements (PPAs) that offset up to 100% of their power use with round-the-clock clean energy (RE-RTC) from solar, wind, and battery storage solutions.
