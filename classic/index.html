II. RE-RTC explained

One day of Sunsure power, on a single dial.

Solar carries the day, wind carries the night, and storage fills every gap, so your plant sees one steady line of green power. Illustrative profile, not a measured output.

Solar0%
Wind0%
Storage0%
0006121824

Circular dial of one day with noon at the top. Each of 24 spokes shows the share of solar, wind and storage for that hour. Solar supplies most power from about 8 am to 5 pm, wind supplies much of the night, and storage fills any remaining gap, so total supply stays at 100 percent all 24 hours.
