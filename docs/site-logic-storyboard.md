# sunsure-energy.com: logic and storyboard of the live homepage

Read on 28 Oct 2026 from the live site (copy, section order, colours, motion libraries, screenshots at 1536px). Nothing in the redesign has been started from this; it is the brief to design against.

## 1. The one-line logic

The page answers one question in a fixed order: can I trust you to deliver round-the-clock green power, at scale, quickly, for a business like mine, and how do I start?

Order of the argument:

1. Promise (hero)
2. Who you are, in numbers (about + stats + investor)
3. Where you operate, as a map (portfolio)
4. Proof that plants exist (plant carousel)
5. What you sell (solutions)
6. Why you over others (advantage)
7. Proof from buyers (testimonials + logos)
8. Proof of expertise (knowledge hub)
9. Ask (contact)

It is a classic B2B trust ladder: claim, credentials, evidence, product, differentiator, social proof, authority, conversion. The weak spot is that the ask only appears at the very end, and the nav button is the only earlier conversion point.

## 2. Audience and intent

- Primary: energy and sustainability heads, procurement and CFOs at large industrial buyers (steel, cement, pharma, FMCG, ceramics, data centres). They already know they want cheaper or greener power; they need confidence in delivery.
- Secondary: investors and partners (Partners Group backing, 400+ million USD raised), job seekers (Careers), press (Media and Awards).
- Buyer's hidden questions the page answers: is it real (plants, MW), is it big enough (GW pipeline, states), who else uses it (80+ customers, named testimonials), how fast (6 months vs 12), what will it cost me (no upfront cost, fixed tariff).

## 3. Storyboard, frame by frame

Heights are from the live page at 1536px wide (about 8,800px in total). Backgrounds are the real colours in use.

### Frame 0. Header (fixed, transparent over hero)
- Logo (S-mark plus wordmark) left; About, Solutions, Projects, Careers, Knowledge Hub; red pill "Contact Us"; LinkedIn icon.
- Dropdowns: About (Company Overview, Media and Awards), Solutions (RE RTC: RTC Power, Energy Storage, Green Attributes; Green Energy Open Access: Hybrid, Solar, Wind PPAs), Careers (Careers at Sunsure, Job Openings), Knowledge Hub (Blogs, Webinars).
- Role: orientation, and one persistent conversion point.

### Frame 1. Hero (about 600px visible, full-bleed photo)
- Photography: wind turbines over farmland at golden hour, warm sunrise grade, white text bottom-left.
- Slide 1: "Making India's Power Move" / creating round-the-clock renewable energy assets, powering India's unstoppable growth.
- Slides 2 to 4 (identical in the CMS): "Power your business green" / developing renewable energy assets, powering India's growth.
- One CTA, red pill: "Explore Our Solutions". Prev/next arrows bottom-right.
- Motion: slider (slick/swiper), fade between photos. Nothing else moves.
- Job: emotion and category. Says who it is for ("your business") and what ("green", "round-the-clock").

### Frame 2. About: "Powering India's energy independence" (dark charcoal #242222, about 1,000px)
- Left: eyebrow "About Sunsure", heading, one paragraph (founded 2014, RE-RTC provider, IPP, PPAs covering up to 100% of use through solar, wind and storage), red text link "Know More".
- Centre: the S-mark rebuilt from two photographs (wind turbines at sunset, engineer in a helmet on a solar field) inside two slanted parallelograms with a circular cut-out at the join. This is the brand's strongest visual idea.
- Right: three stats with red line icons: 400+ million USD raised in equity capital; 80+ happy industrial customers; 800+ MW commissioned.
- Bottom: a raised card strip "We are backed by Partners Group" with a Know More link.
- Job: credibility in one screen. Identity (the mark made of real work), then numbers, then the investor name.

### Frame 3. Portfolio: "Portfolio of distributed renewable energy assets" (pale sky blue #e9f6ff)
- Text left: eyebrow "Our Projects", heading in deep green, one paragraph on C&I customers cutting bottom-line cost, stats 8.3+ GW pipeline and 16+ Indian states, link "See All Projects".

- Faint slanted parallelogram shapes in the background.
- Job: scale and spread. The map does the persuading; the paragraph is secondary.

### Frame 4. Our plants in India (dark charcoal)

### Frame 5. Solutions: "Every kind of business could use the right kind of energy" (pale pink)
- Centred heading, illustrated transformer left and solar panels right.
- Pill tab switch: RE RTC Solutions | Green Energy Open Access (the active tab is red).
- White panel: left a title and accordion (RTC Power, Energy Storage, Renewable Energy Certificates; or Hybrid, Solar, Wind PPAs), right a descriptor, "Know More" and a photograph that changes with the open item.

### Frame 6. The Sunsure Advantage: "Invest in a sustainable future" (pale sky blue)
- Four white cards, each with a red line icon, a faint slanted shape, a red title, and copy revealed on hover: Fastest Power Delivery (about 6 months against about 12); Pan India Power Delivery and Round-the-Clock Energy; In-House Capabilities; Customer Centricity.

### Frame 7. Testimonials and clients (dark charcoal)

## 4. Visual and motion system the page already has

- Rhythm of backgrounds: photo, charcoal, sky blue, charcoal, pink, sky blue, charcoal, mint, photo, black. Dark and light alternate so each section feels like a new scene. Pastel tints plus deep green headings are the "green" signal; red is the action and icon colour.
- Colours in real use: red #fe3a20, charcoal #242222, black, white, deep green #064635 (light-section headings), sky #e9f6ff, mint #ddf0ee, pink tint. Font: Anek Latin throughout.
- The brand angle: the S-mark's two parallelograms recur as the About image, the faint shapes behind light sections, the clipped corner on photos and cards, the quote card corner, and the logo.
- Line icons in red for stats and advantages; no stock icon-pack look.
- Libraries loaded: GSAP and ScrollTrigger, Slick, Owl, Swiper, Lottie (the logo animation), WOW/fadeInUp for section reveals, mCustomScrollbar. Motion that actually shows: hero slider, fade-up on sections, About image and counters, carousels, accordion and tab switching, hover on advantage cards, Lottie logo.
- Heading animation: some headings reveal word by word (visible in screenshots as "Powering ...", "Invest ...").

## 5. What works and should survive the redesign

1. The trust-ladder order. Do not reorder the argument; improve how each step is shown.
2. The S-mark made of real photographs. It is the most ownable thing on the site.
3. The India map with capacity labels, and one plant per slide with the real drone photograph.
4. Named testimonials with titles, and real client logos.
5. Dark and light alternation, red as the only action colour.

## 6. What is weak (design opportunities, content untouched)

1. Hero is a photograph and one button; none of the 6-vs-12-month, 800+ MW or 80+ customers appears until two scrolls later.
2. Hero slides 2 to 4 are identical, so the slider adds no information.
3. The strongest differentiator (6 months vs 12) sits behind a hover on a card, and not at all on touch devices.
4. The portfolio paragraph and the plants carousel say the same thing in two places; map and plants could be one connected moment (click a state, see its plants).
5. Only one conversion point mid-page (nav button); no "get a quote" until the end, and the form there is broken.
6. Section headings animate in a way that leaves them half-visible at rest ("Invest ...", "Portfolio of").
7. Pastel pink and mint sections are the least on-brand tones next to red and charcoal.
8. Content errors to confirm with the client before they are carried into the new design: Pailani Solar shows 30 MWp in the heading and 15 MWp in the text; Solapur 1 is still described as under construction with an estimated March 2025 commissioning; "Sunsure on Socials" is empty.

## 7. Storyboard of the proposed redesign flow (same argument, sharper staging)

Same ten frames in the same order. Changes are staging only:

1. Hero: keep the promise line and CTA; bring the three headline numbers into the first screen; one hero image story instead of four slides.
2. About: keep the two-photograph S-mark; make it the page's signature motion (halves slide together as the stats count up).
3. Portfolio: map becomes interactive, with plants attached to states.
4. Plants: keep one-plant-per-slide drone photography, linked from the map.
5. Solutions: keep the two-bucket structure; show the matching customer names next to each.
6. Advantage: show the 6 vs 12 month claim without needing a hover.
7. Testimonials and logos: keep as is, with better pacing.
8. Knowledge Hub: keep, with filters that work.
9. Contact: a working form, plus a short quote prompt earlier in the page.
10. Footer: unchanged content.
